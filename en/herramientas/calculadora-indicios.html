Pay-discrimination evaluator BETA

Project drafting and/or site supervision by architects · public tender or private commission · based on the PEM
Scope: this tool evaluates a single type of commission — an architect's project drafting and/or site supervision, in a public tender or a direct private-sector commission — comparing the amount offered or awarded against two reference floors (cost and MEF). It is not a general fee calculator and does not replace any fee scale.

Scope of the commission
Commission hours — HME
Estimated hours (calculated)—
Estimated from the PEM, the building type/complexity (G) and the scope of the commission (ΣQ), applying the DM 17/6/2016 formula (CP = V·G·ΣQ·P) and converting the result to hours using the reference rate of €60.5/h. You do not need to enter your own estimate of hours: it is derived from objective facts about the works, not the evaluator's subjective judgement. This is the same method used for the reference case (Ripollet / Sta. Margarida) below.

The MEF floor in this tool is always calculated on the collective-agreement salary, not on the SMI: as a professional activity with a legal practice reserve (architect), the appropriate comparator is equivalent qualified employment, not the national minimum wage.

Comparative references, not binding in Spain: the anti-dumping floor marks the threshold for covering the practice's real costs (without industrial profit) under art. 17 of Spain's Unfair Competition Act (Ley 3/1991); the Directive (EU) 2022/2041 reference applies the wage-adequacy criterion (the INE average wage) to the MEF-floor formula — as a minimum-wage floor, rather than an equivalent-employment one, it sits below the main MEF floor. More cross-checks, not binding in Spain: SEGIPSA is the % of PEM the Spanish General State Administration applies to its own commissioned work; the Italian model (DM 17/6/2016) is the value-based fee in force in Italy; the fixed share is the minimum non-discountable percentage in Italian public tenders — 65% of the fee if ≥€140,000, 80% if below that (art. 41.15-bis of the Codice dei Contratti Pubblici, D.Lgs. 36/2023, amended by D.Lgs. 209/2024) — and is only shown for a public-tender procedure.

The SEGIPSA brackets are verified directly against the text of the Resolution of 11/5/2015 (BOE 27/5/2015) — see Legal Framework. Even so, they are shown for manual reference and are not applied automatically, because SEGIPSA prices commissioned work of the Administration itself, not any commission evaluated here. The G/Q parameters of the Italian DM (Tables Z-1 and Z-2, by category and complexity of the works) are likewise not applied automatically: they require identifying the specific building category in the full official table. Use them to check your own HME estimate or the evaluated amount, not as an automatic calculation of the floor.

This tool is not legal advice or a fee scale. Monetary values (SMI, collective agreement, rates) should be updated periodically in line with CPI. The admissible vehicle for a remuneration floor is state regulation or public-procurement oversight (abnormally low tenders, art. 149 LCSP) — never a professional-association-set table. A result showing an "indicator of discrimination" does not, by itself, prove an infringement: it is a starting point for reviewing the case.
