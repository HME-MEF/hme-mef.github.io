Reference fee band estimator BETA

Project drafting and/or site supervision by architects · based on the PEM (construction budget)
What this is, and isn't: this tool calculates, from the PEM, the building type and the scope of the commission, a band of objective references — a cost floor, an anti-discrimination floor (MEF) and two external value references (SEGIPSA, the Italian model) — for the professional to check their own quote against. It is not a recommended tariff, a fee scale, or a price to charge: each professional sets their own fee freely, and this band is only a starting point for cost analysis. See also the pay-discrimination evaluator, which compares an amount already offered or awarded against the MEF floor.

The band runs from the MEF floor (the absolute anti-discrimination minimum) to the higher of the two external value references. A quote can reasonably fall anywhere within the band, or above it; below the MEF floor there is an indicator of undervaluation.

Origin of each reference

ReferenceNature
MEF floorOpportunity cost — what it would cost to hire an equivalent employee. Anti-discrimination floor.
Cost floorThe practice's production cost (salary + overheads + industrial profit), by analogy with art. 131 RGLCAP.
SEGIPSA% of PEM applied by the Spanish General State Administration to its own commissioned work (BOE 27/5/2015).
Italy (DM 17/6/2016)A value-based fee model in force in Italy, used here only as a comparative reference — see Legal Framework.

The historical architects' fee regulations (1905, 1923, 1977), updated to today's euros, are compared against these references in the historical comparison of regulations.

This tool is not legal advice or a fee scale, and does not replace professional judgement about the specific commission. Monetary values (SMI, collective agreement, rates) should be updated periodically in line with CPI. Each professional sets their own fee freely; this band is a cost and value reference, not a recommended price. See the technical methodology for the origin and status of every input used.
