Reference fee band estimator BETA

Project drafting and/or site supervision by architects · based on the PEM (construction budget)
What this is, and isn't: this tool calculates, from the PEM, the building type and the scope of the commission, a band of objective references — a cost floor, an anti-discrimination floor (MEF) and two external value references (SEGIPSA, the Italian model) — for the professional to check their own quote against. It is not a recommended tariff, a fee scale, or a price to charge: each professional sets their own fee freely, and this band is only a starting point for cost analysis. See also the pay-discrimination evaluator, which compares an amount already offered or awarded against the MEF floor.

The band runs from the MEF floor (the absolute anti-discrimination minimum) to the higher of the two external value references. A quote can reasonably fall anywhere within the band, or above it; below the MEF floor there is an indicator of undervaluation. The dashed lines (in the chart, and the "Comparative references" box) are informative cross-checks, not part of the band and not binding in Spain.

Origin of each reference

ReferenceNatureLine
MEF floorOpportunity cost — what it would cost to hire an equivalent employee. Anti-discrimination floor, with a constitutional basis (art. 14 of the Spanish Constitution). Always calculated on the collective-agreement salary: as a profession with a legal practice reserve, the SMI is not an adequate comparator.Solid
Cost floorThe practice's production cost (salary + overheads + industrial profit), by analogy with art. 131 RGLCAP.Solid
Anti-dumping floorThreshold for covering the practice's real costs, without industrial profit (cost × 1.13 instead of × 1.19). Below it, selling is treated as below-cost under art. 17 of the Ley 3/1991 de Competencia Desleal (Spain's Unfair Competition Act). It is the lower bound of the cost floor, not an independent reference.Dashed
SEGIPSA% of PEM applied by the Spanish General State Administration to its own commissioned work (BOE 27/5/2015).Solid
Italy (DM 17/6/2016)A value-based fee model in force in Italy, used here only as a comparative reference — see Legal Framework.Solid
Italy, fixed share (public commissions only)Non-discountable share in Italian public tenders: 65% of the fee if it is ≥€140,000, 80% if below that (art. 41.15-bis of the Codice dei Contratti Pubblici, D.Lgs. 36/2023, as amended by D.Lgs. 209/2024). Italian regulation, not binding in Spain — a reference for how much margin that system allows.Dashed
