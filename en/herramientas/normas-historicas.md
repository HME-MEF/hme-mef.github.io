## Hourly cost and annual remuneration under the official architects' fee regulations

*This is a translation for reference. The [Spanish version]({{ '/herramientas/normas-historicas/' | relative_url }}) is the authoritative one.*

*Figures in pesetas and euros, updated to 2024, 2025 and 2026.*

---

### 1. Purpose and scope

This document brings together, in pesetas and euros, the hourly cost and
annual remuneration arising from the official architects' fee
regulations ([Royal Decrees of 1905, 1923 and 1977, and the SEGIPSA
Resolution of 2015]({{ '/en/marco-legal/' | relative_url }})),
updated to the three years currently covered by the MEF calculator:
2024, 2025 and 2026. The COAM 2007 indicative scale is excluded, as it
is not an official regulation.

Only RD 2512/1977 sets a price per hour. For 1905 and 1923 the only
remuneration reference is the minimum annual salary for full-time site
supervision; its hourly cost is a working calculation over 1,792 annual
hours. The 2015 SEGIPSA Resolution sets neither an hourly rate nor an
annual remuneration figure.

---

### 2. Key results

- **1977 hour, updated:** 1,000 pesetas/h (€6.01) are equivalent to
  €59.39 in 2024, €60.99 in 2025 and €63.13 in 2026 (provisional).
- **1905 and 1923 minimum salary:** 10,000 and 15,000 pesetas/year are
  equivalent to €43,260 and €38,805 in 2024 terms. The latter is 2%
  above the 2024 annual cost of a collectively-bargained architect
  including social-security contributions (€38,123).
- **1905 and 1923 implicit hourly rate:** the updated annual salaries
  divided by 1,792 h give €24.14 and €21.65 per hour in 2024 (€24.79
  and €22.24 in 2025; €25.66 and €23.02 in 2026) — between 2.5 and 2.7
  times lower than the updated 1977 hour.
- **1977 annual income:** the hourly rate over 1,792 h and a 59.3%
  utilization rate give €63,110 in 2024; its "professional share"
  (50–70%) falls between €31,555 and €44,177.
- **SEGIPSA 2015:** gives neither an hourly nor an annual figure, only
  percentages of the PEM (direct-execution budget) and minimums of
  €5,474 (project) and €4,560 (site supervision).
- **Convergence with personnel cost:** the updated 1923 and 1905
  minimum salaries equal €21.65 and €24.14/h, 1.8% and 13.5% above the
  collective-agreement employer cost (€21.27/h). This is proximity, not
  equality.
- **Comparison layers:** without operating expenses, 1905 and 1923
  converge with the collective-agreement cost plus contributions. With
  expenses, MEF (€26.50/h; €31.08/h in the calculator) falls between
  that cost and the 1977 hour per hour actually worked (€35.22/h),
  which includes overheads and profit.
- **Expenses:** the expenses in this comparison's MEF figure (social
  security and operating costs) amount to 39.6% of the total, within
  the 30–50% range recognised by Tarifa VIII of the 1977 decree.

### 6. Comparison layers against MEF

Figures are ordered by what they include: personnel cost (layer A),
personnel cost plus operating expenses (layer B), and 1977 fees, which
incorporate overheads and profit (layer C). All figures are expressed
per hour over 1,792 h, against the collective-agreement employer cost
(€21.27/h = 100).

(2024 euros).*

|---|---|---|---|

| Collective-agreement salary, Level 1 (salary only) | €28,664 | €16.00 | −24.8% |
| Collective agreement + employer contribution (reference = 100) | €38,123 | €21.27 | — |
| Updated 1923 minimum salary | €38,805 | €21.65 | +1.8% |

| **B. MEF with operating expenses (no profit)** | | | |

| Calculator's MEF, collective-agreement basis (higher overheads) | €55,695 | €31.08 | +46.1% |
| **C. 1977 fees (include overheads and profit)** | | | |
| 1977 hour per hour actually worked (€59.39 × 59.3% utilization) | €63,110 | €35.22 | +65.5% |
| 1977 billable hour (100% occupancy) | €106,425 | €59.39 | +179.2% |
| Calculator's cost floor (figure from its technical methodology) | €80,264 | €44.79 | +110.6% |

**Notes**: the calculator's figures are taken from its technical
methodology (2 October 2026) and are assumed to be in 2024 euros. The
59.3% utilization rate is an industry benchmark (Deltek), not an
official figure. The €9,369 in expenses in this comparison correspond
to professional-liability insurance, premises, software, equipment and
other operating costs.

**Reading**: without operating expenses (layer A), the updated 1923 and
1905 minimum salaries sit very close to the collective-agreement
employer cost: +1.8% and +13.5%. Adding operating expenses (layer B)
raises MEF to €26.50/h (this comparison) or €31.08/h (calculator, with
higher overheads). The 1977 hourly rates (layer C), which include
overheads and profit, sit above: €35.22/h per hour actually worked. MEF
thus falls between personnel cost and the 1977 hour. This comparison's
expenses (SS plus operating costs: €18,828) amount to 39.6% of MEF,
within the 30–50% range recognised by Tarifa VIII of the 1977 decree.

### 8. Methodology and caveats

- Conversion: €1 = 166.386 pesetas.
- Annual hours: 1,792 h, the maximum working hours under the
  engineering and technical-consultancy collective agreement — the same
  as in the [technical methodology]({{ '/en/herramientas/metodologia/' | relative_url }}).
  This is an assumption applied to 1905 and 1923, which do not define an
  annual working schedule.
- The 1905 (10,000 pesetas) and 1923 (15,000 pesetas) salaries are the
  "minimum regulatory rate" for full-time site supervision. Project fees
  and staff salaries are paid separately. At those dates there were no
  social-security contributions comparable to today's, so the relevant
  comparison is with employer cost.
- The 1977 hour is a fee (it includes overheads and profit), not a
  salary. Tarifa VIII recognises expenses of 50%, 40% or 30% depending
  on the R index; the 50–70% split used in Table 2 is this comparison's
  own reading of the text.
- The decree's own updating factor (Fa) starts from January 1977; here
  the 1977 annual average is used instead, which is somewhat lower, so
  the updated figures are conservative.
- The 1905 → 1977 and 1923 → 1977 factors come from a linked historical
  series and are approximate. The factor to 2024 chains the INE's 2021
  to 2024 annual averages starting from 2020.
- **2026 is provisional**: an average inflation rate of +3.5% has been
  assumed. It should be replaced with the actual annual average once the
  INE publishes it.
